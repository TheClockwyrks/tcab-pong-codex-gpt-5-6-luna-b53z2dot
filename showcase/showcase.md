# Carom

Carom is a quick, precise paddle duel played across a dark field of moving
geometry. Choose Solo for a focused duel against the arena AI, or Versus for a
local match at the same keyboard.

Every rally begins with a short serve countdown and grows faster as paddles
meet the ball. The opening is readable; the match point is not. Protect your
side while looking for the angle that sends the ball behind the far paddle.

Spin is the difference between a return and a plan. Move into contact to add
curve, then use the two rotating bars and the walls for bank shots that open
lanes your opponent cannot cover. First to eleven, leading by two, takes the
match.
